❓ How to use SmartBank

SmartBank is a personal money tracker. It does not hold real money or connect to a real bank. Never enter your real bank PIN, card number or passwords here.

🚀 Quick start

  1. Create account: choose any 9-16 digit account number and a 4-6 digit PIN. Remember both. The PIN cannot be recovered.
  2. Log in with your account number and PIN.
  3. Deposit the money you have, then record every expense with its purpose.
  4. Open Savings, Profit and Goals to see how you are doing.
💰 Deposit: add money

Enter how many rupees you received and where it came from (salary, pocket money, gift). The page shows what you deposited today, this month and in total.

🧾 Expenses: record spending
  • Enter the amount, pick a category (Food, Travel, Bills...) and write the purpose, for example "bus ticket".
  • You cannot spend more than your balance.
  • The pie chart shows where your money went. The page also shows your average spending per day and your projected month-end spending.
  • Budgets: set a monthly limit for a category. You get a warning at 80% and when you go over.
  • Wrong entry? Press ✕ to delete an expense.
🏦 Savings: keep money aside
  • Move to savings takes money out of your balance so you are not tempted to spend it.
  • Withdraw brings free savings back to your balance. Money inside goals is locked.
  • The calculator shows how much you can have in the future if you save a fixed amount every month.
📈 Profit: how much you gained

Profit = money deposited − money spent in a month. The savings rate is the percentage of your income you kept. Transfers and savings are not counted as income or spending.

🎯 Goals: save for something

Create a goal (laptop, trip, phone) with a target amount and an optional date. Add money to it whenever you can. The app tells you how much to save per month to reach it on time. Deleting a goal puts its money back into free savings.

🔁 Transfer: send money to another SmartBank account

Enter the receiver's account number, the amount and your PIN. The money leaves your balance and reaches theirs immediately. Check the account number carefully, because a transfer cannot be undone.

⏰ Recurring: automatic monthly entries

Add things that repeat every month, such as salary, rent or a subscription. Choose a day from 1 to 28. They are added automatically the first time you open the app on or after that day.

📄 Statement: full history

Filter your transactions by date and type, and download them as a CSV file that opens in Excel or Google Sheets.

🔒 Safety tips
  • Do not share your PIN with anyone.
  • After 3 wrong PIN attempts the account is locked for 5 minutes.
  • Always click Logout when you use a shared computer.
❌ Close account

Use Close account in the top bar. This permanently deletes your account and all your data. First move or withdraw any money you still want to track. You must enter your PIN and type CLOSE to confirm.

🙋 Common questions

I forgot my PIN. It cannot be recovered. Create a new account.

Why does it say insufficient balance? Your balance is deposits minus expenses, savings and transfers. Deposit more money first.

Why is my profit different from my balance? Balance is all your money right now. Profit is only for one month and ignores savings and transfers.
